OPERATOR PROFILE

Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Specialist

Aditi Sharma is a digital marketing specialist and digital marketing expert who treats every account like a system that should get easier to run over time, not harder. Founders and marketing leads bring her in when they want a plan that holds up in a monthly review, not just a pitch deck.

// ORIGIN LOG

How this started

Aditi Sharma's first marketing work was unpaid and small: a friend's shop needed someone to post on Instagram consistently, and that turned into tracking which posts actually drove footfall. The pattern underneath that curiosity, that most marketing decisions are made on instinct when they could be made on evidence, has shaped every project since.

Six years and more than sixty campaigns later, Aditi Sharma has worked across retail, healthcare, SaaS, hospitality, and professional services. Each vertical runs on a different buying rhythm, and carrying that range forward means fewer assumptions and faster diagnosis on a new account.

Aditi Sharma now operates independently as a digital marketing expert, deliberately keeping the client list short enough that no account is running on autopilot.
