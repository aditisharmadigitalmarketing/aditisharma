ABOUT ME

Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Specialist

Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Specialist works with founders, small teams, and marketing leads who want their online presence to actually bring in business, not just look good on paper. Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Expert has spent more than six years building SEO, content, paid advertising, and email systems that report back in real numbers, so decisions come from evidence rather than habit.

MY STORY

How this started

Aditi Sharma's first marketing work was small and unpaid, managing an Instagram page for a friend's shop out of pure curiosity about why some posts got attention while others disappeared. That curiosity turned into a full career once it became clear how much a clear, tested plan could change a small business, not through luck, but through consistent, measured work. Over the years, Aditi Sharma has worked across retail, healthcare, SaaS, hospitality, and professional services, and each industry has added a different lesson about how people search, compare, and decide before they buy. Today, Aditi Sharma works independently, keeping the client list short enough that no account is ever running on autopilot.

WHAT I BRING

What I bring to a project

Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Specialist blends strategy with hands on execution. Every engagement starts with a proper audit of what is already working and what is quietly costing money, followed by a plan built around the business goals that actually matter, not vanity numbers. Search engine optimisation, content strategy, paid advertising, email marketing, and social media are treated as connected systems rather than separate services, because most businesses lose growth in the gaps between channels, not inside any single one of them. Reporting stays in plain language, with real figures attached, so clients always know what changed and why.

EXPERIENCE

Experience that shapes the work

Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Expert began as a digital marketing associate running small ad budgets and social pages for local businesses, then moved into a senior digital marketing role at an agency, managing multi channel campaigns for retail and SaaS clients. That agency experience, reviewing dozens of accounts across very different budgets, shaped a strong instinct for spotting where a marketing plan is quietly wasting money. Since 2024, Aditi Sharma has worked independently, applying that same instinct directly for a small group of clients who want more attention than a large agency can usually offer.

HOW I WORK

How I work with clients

Communication stays simple and regular, usually through short weekly or biweekly updates, so a project never feels like a black box. Aditi Sharma is upfront when a channel is not performing, even if that means recommending less paid work, because trust matters more than a longer invoice. Budgets, whether large or modest, are spent with the same care as personal money, and every campaign is tracked closely enough to catch a slow week before it becomes a slow quarter.

WHY CLIENTS CHOOSE HER

Why businesses choose to work together

Businesses that hire Aditi Sharma Digital Marketing Specialist are usually tired of marketing that looks busy without moving real numbers like enquiries, bookings, or repeat purchases. What they get instead is a marketing partner who explains decisions clearly, measures everything honestly, and builds for growth that keeps compounding long after the first campaign ends. That combination of strategy, hands on execution, and straightforward communication is what continues to bring founders and marketing teams back, project after project.
